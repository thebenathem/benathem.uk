Process

Simple from first message to launch.

You should know what is happening, what I need from you and what comes next. The process stays intentionally straightforward.

  1. 01

    Scope

    We define what the site needs to do, which pages are required and what content already exists.

  2. 02

    Design

    I establish the visual direction, layout system and responsive behaviour around your actual content.

  3. 03

    Build

    The approved direction becomes a working site, tested across common screen sizes and browsers.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Final checks, domain connection and deployment. You get a site that is ready to use, not a half-finished handover.
